## 🇨🇳 中文

面向制作 2D 赛璐珞或国漫 webtoon 半解说短剧的创作者。用户提供故事、剧本、参考视频或已有素材，流程锁定剧情、资产、清晰度、旁白音色和语音窗口，再按批准分镜生成镜头，核验对白原声并制作统一旁白。交付旁白、保留的对白轨、文本、时间元数据及检查记录，供用户自行剪辑。旁白负责背景、时间跳转和不可直说的内心信息，对白负责冲突、揭示、拒绝、反击和情绪爆点。终点是旁白交接，不负责最终剪辑、视频装配、字幕烧录、背景音乐与音效混音或导出质检，追加生成与整批重做须另行授权。

### ✨ 能做什么

- 项目锁卡一次询问起点、题材、画幅/时长/字幕、清晰度（768P 或 2K）、2D 子模式与旁白音色
- 旁白时长与位置在分镜设计阶段预留并锁定，没有可行旁白窗口的镜头不得生成
- 对白只取视频原声，不生成、不替换、不重配对白语音合成
- 整集旁白在同一批次或统一母轨生成，音色、语速 1.15、音高、响度 -16 LUFS 等参数整集锁定
- 旁白与对白绝不同轨重合，每个语音区间至少留 0.20 秒保护间隔，实测重叠即拒绝混音
- 局部返工只修有证据的脚本行、音频区间与确实失效的镜头，保留合格旁白与上次批准版本

## 🇬🇧 English

For creators making 2D cel or webtoon half-narrated short dramas. You bring a story, script, reference video, or existing assets; the workflow locks plot, assets, resolution, narrator voice, and speech windows, generates shots from the approved storyboard, verifies original dialogue, and produces one consistently voiced narration. It delivers the narration, the preserved dialogue tracks, transcripts, timing metadata, and review notes for user-led editing. Narration carries background, time jumps, and unspeakable inner information, while dialogue carries conflict, reveals, refusals, comebacks, and emotional peaks. The stop point is narration handoff: no final cutting, video assembly, subtitle burn-in, music or SFX mixing, or export QC, and extra generation or whole-batch redo needs separate authorization.

### ✨ What it does

- One project lock card collects start point, genre, aspect ratio/duration, subtitle intent, resolution (768P or 2K), 2D sub-mode, and narrator voice
- Narration length and position are reserved and locked at storyboard stage; a shot with no viable narration window is never generated
- Dialogue comes only from video original audio — never generated, replaced, or re-voiced with synthesis
- Whole-episode narration is generated in one batch or master track, locking voice, 1.15 speed, pitch, and -16 LUFS loudness across the episode
- Narration and dialogue never share a mixed track: every speech interval keeps at least a 0.20s guard band, and any measured overlap is rejected
- Local rework touches only evidenced script lines, audio ranges, and truly broken shots, preserving qualified narration and the last approved version
